The list is in a different order every visit. Each piece is seeded, so reseed gives you a genuinely different variant of the same patch, and copy link captures the one you are looking at. Arrow up and down move through the list. Pieces marked flashing contain rapid strobing. By default the gates are driven by a built in signal; use mic swaps in your own audio, which is how these are performed.
