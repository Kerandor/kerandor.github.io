hydra-synth

Fun feedback and oscillations. Pick a piece and it retunes. The list is in a different order every visit, and arrow up and down move through it. Reseed draws a different variant of the same patch, copy link captures the exact one on screen, and use mic drives the gates from your own audio instead of the built in signal. Pieces marked flashing contain rapid strobing.
